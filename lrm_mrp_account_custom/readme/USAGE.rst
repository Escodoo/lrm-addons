To use this module, you need to:
- Create an BoM for the product and select the Subcontracting BoM type.
- In the `product.template` or `product.product` form view, fill in the Vendor who is the subcontractor in the Purchase tab.
- Click on the button "Calculate Price from Subcontract" on the form view.
- The price will consider the values of components and operations of the BoM plus the subcontract price.
- A server action called "Calculate Price from Subcontract", will also be available in the list or form view.
